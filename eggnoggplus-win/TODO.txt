- Complete secret-free repro bundles with input traces, compatibility manifest,
  extended chaos history, and sequence-aware network measurements.
- Extend long paired and uint32-wrap coverage through native entity lifecycle.

  duplicates, bandwidth, and mixed) through native entity lifecycle. Normal

  Coordinated correction with injected would-block now runs through the

  congestion control, adaptive delay, bounded catch-up, and time sync.
- Add authenticated NAT rebinding, direct-to-relay failover, MTU discovery,
  IPv6, and richer route diagnostics.
