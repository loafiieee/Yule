How rooms work

You author rooms from the center outward. Yule mirrors every outer room on the other side, so two source rooms make a three-room arena.

Faithful previews, safe output

Greggnogg renders from the game’s original data/*.png atlases. Animated, procedural, and physics-driven objects are marked as approximations; package validation follows the Yule loader, not the preview.
